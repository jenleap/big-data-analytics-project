Dataset Overview

This project uses the Instacart Online Grocery Shopping Dataset. It contains over 3 million orders from 206,000 users, covering 49,000 unique products across 21 departments and 134 aisles.

Orders Analysis

Orders vary widely in size. Most customers make small, frequent purchases, while a few place large stock-up orders.

User Behavior

Users exhibit varied engagement and product diversity. Some are highly loyal and exploratory.

Product Popularity

Few staples dominate, while most products are niche — a classic long-tail distribution.
